# Cafe Gator Design Notes

## Colour palette
- Black
- White
- Dark gator green (used ONLY as the gator's skin colour)
- Red (used ONLY for the gator's bow tie and small accents)

## Style
- Hand-drawn cafe aesthetic
- Welcome screen: animated and playful
- Sign-in screen: simple and neat
- Mimicking the nostalgic atmosphere created by cafes and coffee culture
